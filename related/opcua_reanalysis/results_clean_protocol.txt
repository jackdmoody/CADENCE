B. THE THREE PROTOCOL ISSUES
====================================================================================================
  (1) Training normals are scored. Instances 1-10 train the model and are 10 of the 60 evaluated.
      With contamination=c, IsolationForest sets its threshold at the c-quantile of its own training scores,
      so about c*10 of those same normals are flagged by construction (c=0.2 -> 2 FPs).
      contamination 0.1: FP count on the 10 training normals over 200 seeds = {1: 200}
      contamination 0.2: FP count on the 10 training normals over 200 seeds = {2: 200}
      contamination 0.3: FP count on the 10 training normals over 200 seeds = {2: 22, 3: 178}
      contamination 0.4: FP count on the 10 training normals over 200 seeds = {2: 22, 3: 44, 4: 134}

  (2) 'Normal' message size is the mode over all 60 runs, attacks included: 161 bytes.
                                        setpoint_writes  sizes
      client       in_training_normals                        
      192.168.1.16 False                             63  [165]
                   True                               4  [165]
      192.168.1.36 False                            100  [161]
      Mode over training normals only: 165 bytes. The all-runs mode (161) is the UA Expert client's size,
      a client that appears only in attack runs, so 'size deviation' flags the trusted HMI's writes, including
      the four in training normals 6, 8, 9, 10. With the training-only mode it flags UA Expert writes instead.
      3 Sep run: setpoint writes per client {'192.168.1.16': 7, '192.168.1.36': 7}; mode computed on that run alone = 161 (a 7-7 tie, broken by pandas' sort order).

  (3) Contamination chosen by F1 on the evaluation set. Notebook cell 41: 'contamination = 0.4 is chosen as it is
      an optimal value based on F1-score on the evaluation set'; the saved/reported model uses 0.2.
      c=0.05  TP  0/50  FP  1/10  F1 0.000

      c=0.4   TP 50/50  FP  3/10  F1 0.971
      The usable range (F1 > 0) begins at c=0.15; anything below gives recall 0. The choice needs attack labels.
